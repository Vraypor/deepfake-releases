# deepfake

A music player for Windows: YouTube, YouTube Music and SoundCloud, your own files, lyrics, Discord status and more.

## Install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/Vraypor/deepfake-releases/releases/latest) and download `deepfake-setup-….exe`.
2. Run it. Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"** because the installer isn't signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. On first start, deepfake asks to download yt-dlp and ffmpeg (about 400 MB). Click **Download**.

## Updates

deepfake checks for updates on start and every few hours. When one is out it asks; click install and it restarts on the new version.

## If YouTube won't play

YouTube sometimes asks apps to prove they aren't bots. In deepfake's settings, under **YouTube account**, click **Sign in** and use a spare Google account; it plays again.
